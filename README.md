# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee data and provide valuable insights into workforce trends, employee attrition, job satisfaction, salary distribution, and demographic information. The dashboard enables HR professionals to make data-driven decisions by identifying patterns that impact employee retention.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of this project is to:

- Analyze employee attrition trends.
- Understand workforce demographics.
- Monitor key HR metrics.
- Identify factors influencing employee turnover.
- Support strategic HR decision-making.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- 👥 Total Employees
- 🚪 Employee Attrition
- 📉 Attrition Rate (%)
- 💰 Average Salary
- 🎂 Average Employee Age
- ⏳ Average Years at Company
- 👨‍💼 Gender Distribution
- 📊 Attrition by Age Group
- 😊 Job Satisfaction Analysis
- 🎓 Attrition by Education Field
- 📅 Attrition by Years at Company
- 🏢 Department-wise Employee Distribution

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- Employee attrition rate is **16.12%**.
- Employees aged **26–35** show the highest attrition.
- Research & Development has the largest workforce.
- Job satisfaction varies across different job roles.
- Employees with fewer years in the company are more likely to leave.
- Education background also influences attrition trends.
